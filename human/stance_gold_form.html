В каждой карточке заметки о том, что советует одна книга (название скрыто), и одно утверждение. Ответьте, что эта книга сказала бы читателю об утверждении, только по заметкам.
A = советует. B = советует против (критикует это или советует другой путь в той же ситуации). N = не затрагивает. U = по заметкам не понять.
«С оговорками»: позиция только для названных людей, времён или ситуаций. Для A и B укажите номер заметки, например t3 или m12.

t7. Destroy egoism at once, giving it no respite through thoughts of sense-objects, and stay quiet and free from duality. (applies to: seekers and yogis; argues against: egoism and thinking on sense-objects)
t8. Renounce selfish actions and burn away thought of sense-objects to break the chain of rebirth. (applies to: seekers and the renunciant; conditions: the actions renounced are those done with selfish motives; argues against: attachment to sense-objects)
t9. Avoid lust, anger, greed, arrogance, spite, envy and jealousy, and give up pride of caste, family and station. (applies to: all seekers; argues against: lust, anger, avarice, arrogance, spite, envy, pride of caste)
t10. Stay the same in mind whether honored or tormented, and be neither grieved nor elated by, attached to, nor averse to sense-objects; stay dispassionate. (applies to: seekers aspiring to liberation in life)
t11. Be satisfied with the bliss of the Self and live happily in it, drinking joyfully of it with senses restrained. (applies to: disciples and seekers; conditions: held with steady body and restrained senses; argues against: dualistic conceptions; seeking happiness in other things)
t12. Abide in faith, devotion and meditation, never be careless in your steadfastness, and carefully concentrate the mind. (applies to: seekers and knowers; those with remaining past-action residue; conditions: keep meditating watchfully while any residue of past action remains; argues against: ignorance and carelessness about one's real nature)
t13. Learn from a knower of the truth, listen attentively, then reflect and meditate yourself, deciding for yourself what is Self and not-Self. (applies to: disciples and seekers; argues against: perverted argumentation)
t14. Do not get lost in scripture or repeating a word; seek direct realization of the Self. (applies to: the wise and seekers; argues against: scriptural erudition, mere verbal repetition, perverted argument)
t15. Strive personally and by every means to be free from repeated birth and death. (applies to: the wise; argues against: relying on others for liberation)
t16. Give up fear; a means of crossing the sea of relative existence exists. (applies to: the qualified disciple; conditions: the seeker has sought refuge, thirsts for liberation, obeys scripture and has a pacified mind)
t17. Eliminate the objective world by reasoning and see the oneness of the individual and the supreme, using rejection and retention of meanings together. (applies to: seekers; conditions: neither wholesale rejection nor wholesale retention works; argues against: wholesale rejection or retention of terms)
t18. Help others out of trouble without any motive, as the good and magnanimous do. (applies to: the magnanimous)
t19. Strengthen discrimination and renunciation first, and carefully purify the mind. (applies to: seekers of liberation; argues against: a mind tainted by restless and dull qualities)
